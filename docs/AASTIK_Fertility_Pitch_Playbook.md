# Aastik Fertility: Pitch Playbook

## 0. The honest answer to "If I meet an investor tomorrow…"

**Don't ask for ₹10 Cr, and don't pitch a hospital.** Two things in your materials would end a meeting tomorrow: casino spam on aastikayurveda.com, and unverifiable claims ("10,000+ patients", the "blind couple" video). An investor will find both within 5 minutes of Googling you.

**Two meetings you can hold now:**

| Meeting | When | Ask | Outcome you want |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. "Pilot partner" meeting**: incubator (Venture Center Pune), SISFS, MSInS, Kapiva Innovation Fund, 2–3 healthcare angels | **After fixes 1–4 below (about 2–3 weeks)** | **₹15–25 L** as grants plus an F&F/angel iSAFE (cap ₹6–10 Cr) | Pilot funded; angels committed to follow on at the gate |
| **B. Pre-seed pitch**: IPV, Venture Catalysts, Rebalance, All In Capital, iSeed, Incubate Fund Asia, Endiya | **Months 10–14, after the 9-month gate** | **₹2.5–4 Cr at ₹12–20 Cr pre-money** | Term sheet |

For meeting A, the pitch is **"Back the experiment."** For meeting B, it is **"Here is the data."** The narrative below fits both. Only the traction slide and the ask change.

## 2. The one-line pitch

> **"Aastik Fertility is the accountable Ayurveda fertility clinic. Couples are treated together, procedures are used only when indicated, every outcome is published with its denominator, and a written rule tells you when to see an IVF specialist. That rule is why gynaecologists refer to us instead of warning patients away."**

Avoid: "natural pregnancy", "conceive without IVF", "detox", "improves egg quality", "₹1,000 Cr", "India's first". **Gynoveda's own pages use the first four.** Your edge is being visibly more careful.

## 4. How to run the meeting (30 minutes)

| Minutes | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | One-line pitch. Then say: "I'll show you the problem, how we're different, our safety system, and exactly what we'll prove with your money." |
| 2–5 | A **couple story**: an anonymised composite, or a real case with consent. Pick one where you **referred to IVF early**. That one story disarms the biggest objection. |
| 5–15 | Slides 2–7 |
| 15–20 | Slides 9–12: traction, honest competition, team, the ask |
| 20–30 | Questions. Bring the printed answers to the 20 tough questions. |

**Raise the bad news yourself:**
- "Our old website had spam injected. Here's the incident note and the clean site."
- "The evidence for Ayurveda in infertility is weak. That's why we built the registry."
- "Gynoveda is 5 km away. Here's why couples choose us."

Investors trust founders who name the risks first.

**Bring:** a one-page data room index (MIS, incorporation documents, SOPs, LOIs, COAs, claims register, pilot plan), the deck PDF, and a one-page financial summary.

## 5. Phrases to use and to avoid

| Avoid | Use instead |
|---|---|
| "Body detox before pregnancy" | "A 90-day couple preparation programme" |
| "Conceive naturally without IVF" | "Structured care before IVF, with a clear point at which we refer you" |
| "Improves egg / sperm quality" | "We track ovulation, semen parameters and metabolic markers, and report them" |
| "10,000+ patients" | A number you can prove |
| "₹1,000 Cr company" | "₹10–15 Cr revenue in 3–4 years across 6–10 sites, with published outcomes" |
| "We'll build a 10-bed hospital" | "Outpatient hub and consult satellites. Beds add no value in fertility care, and insurance excludes infertility." |
| "Guaranteed" / "success rate" | "Here is our denominator." |

## 6. Outreach sequence

1. **Weeks 1–3:** fixes 1–4. Incorporate. Apply for DPIIT recognition.
2. **Weeks 3–5:** apply to Venture Center Pune (incubation and SISFS route) and to MSInS; brief the Kapiva Innovation Fund on funding the registry.
3. **Weeks 4–6:** coffee meetings with 5–8 healthcare operator angels in Pune (OBGs, hospital promoters). Ask for **advice first, then a small iSAFE cheque**.
4. **Weeks 6–8:** close the ₹15–25 L Stage 0 round. Start the pilot.
5. **Month 9:** gate review. Send a monthly investor update from month 1 onward. Consistent updates are what turn angels into pre-seed leads.
6. **Months 10–14:** meeting B with IPV, Venture Catalysts, Rebalance, All In Capital, iSeed, Incubate Fund Asia and Endiya. **Do not approach Fireside or India Alternatives first; both back Gynoveda.**
