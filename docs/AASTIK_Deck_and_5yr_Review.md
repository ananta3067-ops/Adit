### 2.2 CFO stress test: what holds and what will be attacked

**What holds well**
1. **Staging is excellent.** T1 alone reaches the PK readout with ≥ ₹2 Cr cash in every scenario. The M4 lab gate (dissolution shows a ≥ 15-min lag) can save the ₹1.15 Cr PK spend before it starts. Investors rarely see this discipline at seed.
2. **The per-strip waterfall is now correct** (GST removed before trade margin).
3. **Pre-registered pass mark** (1.25×, lower 90% CI > 1.0), with five written kill criteria.
4. **Honest framing** that the core idea is unproven, with comparable deals sourced (OZiva ~4–6.5×, Wellbeing ~9.3×).

**What will be attacked, and the fix**

| # | Issue | Why it matters | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | **Base EBITDA 43–53% (FY30–32)** | Branded nutraceutical companies at ₹50–100 Cr typically run well below this. Head office is modelled near-flat (₹7.5 L/month core + admin) while the field force grows to 75 MRs. | Add a **"realistic base"**: grow head office as you scale (zonal managers, finance head, regulatory/QA team, warehouse, digital marketing). Target **25–32% EBITDA in FY32**. Lead with conservative, then realistic base. |
| P2 | **45 doctors × 45 strips per MR is founder judgement, not data** | Drives the base case almost entirely | Test it on Yronik prescription data now. Set the M7–M9 check (first 3 MRs ≥ 1,200 strips/month) as a visible milestone. |
| P3 | **Actuals are all zero** (opening cash, Yronik revenue, balance sheet, cap table) | An investor's first request is "send last 2 years' financials". A model with zero actuals looks unfinished. | Get the CA's FY25/FY26 balance sheet, sales ledger and cap table. Load them into the model before any meeting. |
| P4 | **FY28→FY29 revenue jumps ~9×** (₹1.16 → ₹10.2 Cr) | Looks like a hockey stick | Show the monthly bridge: MRs × active doctors × strips. Show that ~60% of FY29 comes from MRs hired in FY28. |
| P5 | **SOM needs national reach.** FY32 base ≈ 3,375 active prescribers (75 × 45) vs ~11,800 core prescribers in the 4 launch states (≈ 29%). | Too high a share of launch states | State on the market slide that the base case needs expansion beyond 4 states from FY30. |
| P6 | **Working capital** ≈ ₹7.8 L per mature MR → **≈ ₹5.9 Cr at 75 MRs** | Can strain cash even in a profitable year | Show net working capital as its own line in the cash flow. Confirm the base case funds it internally. |
| P7 | **GST 18% vs 5%** | +12% net revenue if 5% | One-line written confirmation from your CA. Keep 18% in the base until then. Note: the Channels sheet note "likely CDSCO drug" contradicts the FSSAI route, so delete that wording. |
| P8 | **COGS ₹50 is unquoted** (older deck says ₹40, Gamma deck says ₹70) | Three numbers for one cost is a credibility hit | Get a written CMO quote. Use one number everywhere. |
| P9 | **VC return maths.** ₹200–400 Cr exit = 1.6–3.2× for the investor | Most VC funds need a credible ≥ 10× path | Pitch angels, family offices, strategics and government-linked funds first. With VCs, show the platform (Boswellia, berberine, licensing) as the route to ₹1,000 Cr+, and say clearly it is unproven. |
| P10 | **Downside is still short ≈ ₹1.54 Cr** by FY32 | Investors check survival | Load Yronik actuals. If still short, show the cost-cut plan that closes it. |
