# Aastik Fertility: Investor Review

**Note on valuations:** Indian pre-seed and seed rounds rarely disclose valuations. The pre-money ranges below are **inferred** from round size and standard dilution of 10–25%. They are not quoted comparables. FX ≈ ₹88/$.

## 1. Would I invest?

**Today: no equity cheque.**
- There is no fertility revenue yet.
- The ₹2 L/month clinic figure is unverified.
- There is no CAC or outcome data.
- The whole programme depends on one MD.
- The category leader, Gynoveda (~$11 M raised), appears flat at about ₹68 Cr revenue (aggregator data).
- Diligence would also flag the casino spam on aastikayurveda.com (it hurts any brand the new company licenses) and claims risk (the DMR Act and the YouTube video's title).

**After the 9-month gate: yes, a small pre-seed cheque, if all of these are true:**
1. **40–50 couples enrolled and paying** (not just deposits), with 15 or more in the last 3 months.
2. **Effective CAC of ₹15 k or less** (no more than 20% of the ticket) and falling; consult-to-enrolment of **25% or more**.
3. Drop-out and refunds of **15% or less**.
4. **100% compliance with the referral clock**, audited by the visiting OBG.
5. **Zero serious adverse events.**
6. Outcomes registry at least **90% complete**, with the CTRI/ethics protocol filed and 6-month outcomes reported with denominators.
7. **At least 5 active OBG/IVF partners**, and at least 25% of enrolments coming from professional referrals.
8. A **Prasuti Tantra MD or OBG** on the team, and at least 50% of follow-ups done by non-founder doctors.
9. **Marketing that is clean** under ASCI, the DMR Act and PCPNDT: website, Google listing and video.

**What would end it:** growth driven mostly by Meta ads with rising CAC (the Gynoveda trap), or any case where IVF was delayed for a woman over 35.

## 4. Structure and instruments

- **Use a Pvt Ltd, not an LLP.** An LLP has no share capital, so it cannot issue CCPS, iSAFEs or ESOPs. FDI into an LLP is allowed only under the automatic route without performance conditions, and most networks and VCs will not invest in one ([India Briefing](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-business-entity-private-limited-llip-opc-guide-39644.html/); [iPleaders](https://uatblog.ipleaders.in/fdi-llps-vs-private-limited-companies/)).
- **DPIIT recognition** immediately after incorporating. SISFS requires it, plus incorporation less than 2 years before applying ([myScheme](https://myscheme.gov.in/schemes/sisfs-fs)); so does the 80-IAC tax holiday. Angel tax was abolished from FY25 ([KPMG](https://kpmg.com/in/en/blogs/2024/08/abolishment-of-angel-tax-a-welcome-move.html)), but you still need a registered-valuer report for each share issue.
- **Instruments:**
  - **Stage 0:** an iSAFE structured as CCPS (100X.VC template; [Treelife](https://treelife.in/legal/isafe-notes-in-india/)), with a ₹6–10 Cr cap and a 15–20% discount.
  - **Pre-seed:** a priced CCPS round.
  - **Avoid statutory convertible notes.**
  - **Seed:** 1× non-participating liquidation preference, broad-based weighted-average anti-dilution, one investor board seat.
- **Cap table:**
  - Dr. Soni holds shares **personally**, not through Aastik Ayurveda Pvt Ltd. That avoids cross-holding and conflicts.
  - No more than 15 direct angels; route larger groups through a syndicate SPV.
  - Sign a shareholders' agreement from Stage 0.
- **Founder vesting:** 4 years with a 1-year cliff, with credit for the pilot period and acceleration on change of control. **Hire a full-time operating co-founder or COO before seed.**
- **ESOP pool:** 8–10% before pre-seed, rising to 12–15% before Series A.
- **Doctor partners:** ESOPs with 4-year vesting, or site-level profit share paid as salary or bonus ([Pluro precedent](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/pluro-fertility-bags-inr-125-cr-in-series-a-led-by-bessemer/499003)). **Never give equity or payments to referring OBG/IVF partners or to ISKCON.** Referral commissions are prohibited under the ISM and NMC ethics codes.
- **Related-party agreements with Aastik Ayurveda** need board and shareholder approval (Companies Act s.188) and must be in the data room:

| Agreement | Terms investors will expect |
|---|---|
| Brand | Ideally **assign the "Aastik Fertility" mark** to the new company. Otherwise a licence of 10+ years for 1–2% of net revenue or a fixed fee. It must survive change of control and the licensor's insolvency, and include QC, cross-default and reputational kill-switch clauses. |
| Medicines | Non-exclusive supply at arm's-length prices benchmarked to third-party GMP quotes, with batch COAs, freedom to dual-source and a most-favoured price |
| Katraj NVCC site | A sublease or service agreement **with ISKCON's consent as head landlord**, at market rent, with lock-in and exit terms. Patient records and registry data belong to the new company (DPDP). |

## 6. The first five metrics investors will diligence

| # | Metric | Gate (month 9) | Seed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Blended CAC and payback, by channel | ≤₹15 k, falling | <₹12 k; ≥40% organic or referral |
| 2 | Consult-to-enrolment; completion and refunds | ≥25%; drop-out ≤15% | ≥30%; completion ≥85% |
| 3 | Outcomes with denominators: 6-month clinical pregnancy rate by cohort, registry completeness, referral-clock compliance, serious AEs | ≥90% complete; 100% compliance; 0 serious AEs | Two published cohort reports |
| 4 | Professional referral share and active partners | ≥25%; ≥5 partners | ≥35%; ≥20 partners |
| 5 | Site contribution margin and founder independence | ≥40% after CAC; ≥50% of follow-ups by non-founder doctors | Mature-site EBITDA ≥20%; founder in <20% of consults |

Also tracked: medicine and repeat revenue per couple, and NPS (target 50 or more).

**Bottom line:** do not raise equity yet. Run the pilot on grants and founder money. Incorporate a clean Pvt Ltd now. Come back in months 10–14 asking for **₹2.5–4 Cr at ₹12–20 Cr pre-money**. The outcomes registry and the referral clock are what make this fundable, so put them ahead of growth.
