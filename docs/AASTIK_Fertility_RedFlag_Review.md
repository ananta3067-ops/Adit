# Aastik Fertility: Red-Flag Review

**Framing:** Aastik Fertility is a new, separate entity with separate investors, unrelated to the curcumin venture. Founder bandwidth and related-party risk with Aastik Ayurveda Pvt Ltd still apply.

**Bottom line:** I would not invest today. There is no fertility revenue, no outcome data and no CAC data. The plan's own year-1 model shows operating contribution of **−₹1.3 L** and an effective CAC of **₹23.8 k**, about 35% of the ₹67.6 k average ticket, against its own 20% gate. Any investor who reads the founder's file will find these numbers.

---

## 1. Top 12 red flags

### CRITICAL

**1. The website carries casino spam, and its trust claims cannot be verified.**
- **Problem:** All 12 posts on aastikayurveda.com/blog are multilingual online-casino articles, attributed to the account "Sneha Loni". That points to a compromised WordPress account or paid-link injection. The site also claims "10,000+ Patients" for a centre that opened in Feb 2026, shows "0+" counters, and has a meaningless "5.0+ Patient Reviews" badge.
- **How an investor finds it:** By Googling the brand, in about 5 minutes.
- **Mitigation:** Clean and harden the site (2FA, plugin audit, Search Console removals) and write an incident note. Substantiate or reword the 10,000 figure. **If the spam is still live at the first meeting, the deal is dead.** It signals weak operational and IT control in a business that will hold reproductive health data.

**2. Harm from delaying IVF creates outcome liability.**
- **Problem:** Every month an Ayurveda programme keeps a woman of 35+ or with low AMH away from ART lowers her chance of a live birth.
- **Evidence:**
  - A Human Reproduction 2020 model estimates that a 12-month delay cuts live births by about 22% at age 40–42 and about 0.9% under 30 ([OUP](https://academic.oup.com/humrep/article/36/3/666/5998652)).
  - Indian consumer commissions treat false assurances of fertility success as deficiency in service ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/consumer-cases/false-assurances-regarding-ivf-success-is-deficiency-in-service-and-unfair-trade-practice-ernakulum-district-commission-297388); [Medical Dialogues, ₹15 L refund order](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/health/medico-legal/fertility-clinic-doctors-told-to-refund-rs-15-lakh-pay-rs-1-lakh-compensation-for-surrogacy-failure-112488)).
- **How an investor finds it:** A clinical adviser reviews the SOPs and asks to see the referral log.
- **Mitigation:** A signed referral-clock SOP, an audited compliance log at 100%, professional indemnity cover, and a co-management agreement with a reproductive endocrinologist (REI).

**3. The evidence is thin, and the core service cannot be advertised as a remedy.**
- **Evidence base:**
  - The Cureus 2024 systematic review found 14 studies with 248 patients.
  - The Uttarbasti tubal-blockage studies have about 16 patients each.
  - The ashwagandha trials have 46 and 76 patients, largely from one research group.
- **Legal limit:** Under the DMR Act 1954, "sterility in women" and "impotence" are scheduled conditions.
- **Enforcement is live:**
  - ASCI's 2024–25 report found **56% of the 370 healthcare ads it examined misleading** and referred **233 ads to the Ministry of AYUSH** under the DMR Act ([Medical Dialogues](https://medicaldialogues.in/news/industry/pharma/asci-flags-233-health-ads-for-dmr-act-violation-reports-to-ayush-ministry-149226); [ASCI report](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Digital-ASCI-Annual-Complaints-Report-2024-25.pdf)).
  - ASCI has upheld complaints against fertility clinics claiming guaranteed success ([ASCI Jan 2020](https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/asci_jan_2020-_ccc_press_release.pdf)).
- **Rule 170's removal does not help:** omitting Rule 170 only removed state pre-approval of ads. The self-declaration regime, the DMR Act and the CCPA guidelines still apply ([LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/amp/top-stories/supreme-court-vacates-stay-on-ayush-ministrys-notification-that-permitted-advertisements-of-ayurvedic-unani-siddha-drugs-300536); [Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/amp/story/news/misleading-medical-ads-case-supreme-court-closes-case-allows-ayush-ads-without-state-approval)).
- **Unverified:** no ASCI or CCPA ruling naming Gynoveda was found.
- **Mitigation:** A counsel-reviewed claims register and a CTRI-registered outcomes registry.

**4. There is no verified financial base.**
- **Problem:** The ₹2 L/month figure is founder-supplied. The clinic is about 9 months old and fertility revenue is zero.
- **Precedent:** Mojocare's investors uncovered irregularities after it had raised $20.6 M ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/06/18/mojocare-investors-uncover-financial-irregularities-unsustainable-business-model)). Investors now reconcile revenue against bank statements.
- **Mitigation:** Bank-reconciled MIS, GST returns and an EMR patient count. **If the numbers cannot be reconciled, the deal is off.**

### HIGH

**5. A funded competitor sits 4–5 km away.**
- **Problem:** Gynoveda runs 3 Pune clinics, including Kondhwa/Lulla Nagar about 4–5 km from Katraj. It offers a ₹2,000 couple consultation, sells a ₹4,125 three-month pack, has 50+ clinics nationally with 200+ planned, and has 27 k+ reviews. Nova and Oasis are also in Kondhwa.
- Aastik's ₹39 k–₹1.25 L ladder is 10–30× Gynoveda's entry ticket in the same catchment.
- **Mitigation:** Win-loss data from the deposit test.

**6. The category's growth ceiling is already visible.**
- **Evidence:**
  - Gynoveda has raised about $11 M. It reported ₹67.2 Cr revenue in FY24 (loss ₹3.1 Cr) and ₹68.1 Cr in FY25, roughly +1% ([Inc42](https://inc42.com/company/gynoveda/captable/); [TheCompanyCheck](https://www.thecompanycheck.com/company/gynoveda-femtech-private-limited/U85300MH2019PTC327679); unaudited).
  - **The Ayurveda Co shut down in Sep 2026 after raising ₹125 Cr**, citing over-expansion ([YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2026/09/the-ayurveda-co-shuts-down-after-raising-125-cr-founder-says-it-expanded-too-fast)).
  - Investors have become more selective ([TechCrunch, Dec 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/27/india-startup-funding-hits-11b-in-2025-as-investors-grow-more-selective)).
- **Mitigation:** A bottom-up Pune SAM built from gynaecologist referral volumes.

**7. Unit economics are fragile.**
- **Problem:** Year-1 contribution is −₹1.3 L and CAC is about ₹23.8 k. Medicine COGS of ₹700 per person per month is unvalidated, the GST treatment of bundled medicines is unresolved, and the funnel rates are guesses. Performance ads are legally constrained.
- **Mitigation:** Real cohort CAC by channel, plus a CA opinion on GST.

**8. The founder is a single point of dependency, and there are credential gaps.**
- **Problem:** The founder's MD is in Rasa Shastra, not Prasuti Tantra/Stree Roga. The other two doctors are still completing their MD (Manasa Roga). There is no OBG. The founder also runs the existing clinic and product line.
- **Mitigation:** A signed OBG and Prasuti Tantra consultant, a full-time CEO or COO or a written time commitment, and key-person insurance.

**9. Related-party and structural risk.**
- **Problem:** The new company would depend on assets owned by Aastik Ayurveda Pvt Ltd: the premises, the Google listing, the medicines and licences, and the staff. Without arm's-length terms, the new company can be hollowed out through transfer pricing.
- **Mitigation:** Board-approved supply, sublease, services and trademark agreements at audited rates, ROFR and non-compete clauses, and the ISKCON lease's permission to sublease and run clinical procedures.

**10. Product safety: bhasma and heavy metals.**
- **Evidence:** JAMA 2008 (Saper) found metals in 20.7% of Ayurvedic products bought online and **40.6% of rasa shastra products** ([ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2008/08/080826190850.htm)). Published lead-poisoning cases involve bhasmas ([PMC3850721](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3850721/); [BCMJ](https://bcmj.org/bccdc/heavy-metal-poisoning-ayurvedic-medicines)).
- **Why it matters here:** the patients are trying to conceive, or are in early pregnancy.
- **Unverified:** no Aastik SKU has been confirmed to contain bhasma.
- **Mitigation:** NABL heavy-metal COAs for every batch, and **no metallic bhasmas in TTC or pregnancy protocols**.

### MEDIUM

**11. Uttarbasti procedure risk.** Uttarbasti carries infection and trauma risk, and its efficacy evidence is single-arm. SOPs list infection needing antibiotics as a complication ([Amrita SOP](https://emap.ay.amrita.edu/?p=33022)). Unverified: no Indian adverse-event series was found. **Mitigation:** Sterile SOPs, an adverse-event log, and an overuse audit triggered when more than 40% of women receive it.

**12. Brand positioning, regulation and scalability.**
- **Brand:** A religious-campus setting, Garbh Sanskar links (which have a pseudoscience history, per [The Wire](https://science.thewire.in/law/rss-kolkata-super-baby/)) and the "blind couple" video all risk hereditary-outcome implications.
- **Regulation:**
  - The **Maharashtra Clinical Establishments Bill 2026** was introduced on 3 Jul 2026 and is with a Joint Select Committee, which has received 2,000+ suggestions. It would make registration universal and require rate display in three languages, and an interim rate-display circular already applies ([PRS](https://prsindia.org/bills/states/the-maharashtra-clinical-establishments-registration-and-regulation-bill-2026); [FPJ](https://www.freepressjournal.in/mumbai/maharashtra-clinical-establishments-bill-2026-gets-over-2000-suggestions-doctors-seek-relief-for-small-clinics)).
  - The **DPDP Rules 2025** were notified in Nov 2025, with full obligations due around May 2027 ([Bar & Bench](https://www.barandbench.com/view-point/meity-notifies-final-digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025)).
- **Scalability:** a doctor-led model caps out at about 110–120 active couples per 2 rooms and 1 doctor.

## 3. Deal-breakers (a "no" regardless of the pitch)

1. Spam or malware is still live, or the 10,000+ claim is unsubstantiated.
2. Revenue cannot be reconciled to bank statements and GST.
3. There is no audited IVF referral clock, or any case is found of a woman aged 35+ or with low AMH being held back.
4. Any marketing claims cure, conception rates, "no IVF" positioning, or the child's health or sex.
5. Metals appear without batch NABL COAs, or bhasmas are used in TTC or pregnancy protocols.
6. There are no arm's-length related-party agreements, or the IP, listing and licences stay with the old company.
7. There is no qualified OBG or Prasuti Tantra clinician in clinical governance.
8. The founder will not commit defined time and there is no plan to hire an operator.
9. The ask is for beds or hospital capex before the month-9 gate is met.
