## 2. What the company does

Aastik Ayurveda is a **science-led Ayurvedic and nutraceutical products company that operates on a distribution model.**

- **We own the formulations and brands.** We develop standardised Ayurvedic and nutraceutical products with modern dosage forms (capsule-in-capsule, sustained release, enteric-coated).
- **We do not run our own factory.** Products are made by licensed **WHO-GMP contract manufacturers**, and we buy finished goods from them.
- **We sell through distributors, pharmacies, doctors' clinics and institutions.** Our medical representatives create demand among doctors; our distribution partners deliver stock to the market.

Our main cash need is therefore **working capital**: buying stock from manufacturers in minimum order quantities, holding inventory, and giving credit to distributors and institutions until they pay.

## 4. Business model and supply chain

**How goods and money flow:**

1. **WHO-GMP contract manufacturer** makes the product to our formula and specification.
2. **Aastik Ayurveda** (brand owner) buys the finished goods and holds stock in its own or a C&F warehouse.
3. Stock is sold to:
    - **distributors / stockists**, who supply pharmacies and chemists
    - **doctors' clinics** (on GST invoice)
    - **institutions** (Ayurveda colleges and hospitals)
    - **our own clinic** in Pune, and **online** (planned)

- **Demand creation:** medical representatives (MRs) visit doctors, run CMEs and sample programmes. Tracked through the company's own CRM (AASTIK TECH™).
- **Current distribution partners:** Sanchomee Ayurveda (Pune / Mumbai), Panchajanya Pharma (Kota).
- **Institutional supply:** SDM College of Ayurveda, Hassan; SDM Ayurveda College, Udupi.
- **States active:** Rajasthan, Karnataka, Maharashtra.
- **Quality:** each batch comes with a Certificate of Analysis; QR-code batch traceability.

### Why we need working capital (cash cycle)
| Stage | Typical days | Cash effect |
|---|---|---|
| Pay manufacturer (advance or credit) | 30 days credit (target) | Cash out |
| Hold stock (MOQ about 10,000 strips per product) | 90 days | Cash locked in inventory |
| Credit to distributors / institutions | 30–60 days (institutions can be 90) | Cash locked in receivables |
| **Net cash cycle** | **about 90–120 days** | Needs a working-capital limit |

With 10 products, each with its own minimum order quantity, inventory alone needs significant funds before sales are collected.
